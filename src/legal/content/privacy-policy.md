# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy is the current source-of-truth privacy notice for infraflow.

## Information We Collect

We collect account information, workspace information, diagrams, deployment metadata, connected account metadata, and operational data needed to provide the service.

## Connected AWS and GitHub Data

When you connect AWS or GitHub, infraflow stores the minimum connection metadata needed to provide repository sync, infrastructure deployment, resource insights, and account status.

## How We Use Information

We use information to authenticate users, provide dashboards, generate infrastructure artifacts, sync repositories, operate deployments, improve reliability, and secure the platform.

## Security

We use access controls and encrypted secret storage patterns where applicable. You should only grant the permissions required for your intended workflows.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy over time. The version and effective date shown on this page identify the currently effective policy.
